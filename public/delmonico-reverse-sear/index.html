Method

  1. Pat dry, salt generously on all sides with kosher salt, set uncovered on a rack in the fridge.
  2. Preheat oven to 225°F.
  3. Insert the Combustion probe sideways, tip dead center. Set target to 137°F.
  4. Steak on rack, into the oven, straight from the fridge. No need to bring it to room temp.
  5. Pull when the probe reads 125°F, about 45 to 60 minutes.
  6. Rest ten minutes while the heaviest pan gets ripping hot.
  7. Thin film of high-smoke-point oil. Sear 60 to 90 seconds per side.
  8. Add a knob of butter, baste for the last 30 seconds.
  9. Remove, dust Bullock’s seasoning over both sides. Pepper now.
  10. Rest five minutes. Slice against the grain, serve.

Seasoning notes

Salt: kosher salt, about 1/2 tsp Diamond Crystal per pound (1/4 tsp if Morton or table salt). Flaky finishing salt is too coarse to dissolve in a two-hour brine, save it for the table.

Bullock’s Prime Rib Steak Seasoning: an additive, not a substitute for the salt. Salt is only its fourth ingredient, so it under-salts on its own, and its cane sugar and dehydrated garlic scorch in the sear pan. It goes on after the sear, while the steak rests, or a pinch in the butter baste.

Why 137°F: a thick-cut ribeye’s fat renders best around 137°F. The pull is 10 to 15° below target, the sear and rest carry it the rest of the way.

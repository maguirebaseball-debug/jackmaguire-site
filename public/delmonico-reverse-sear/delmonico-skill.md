# Delmonico Reverse Sear

Cook a thick-cut Delmonico (boneless ribeye, about 1.5 inches) by reverse sear. Final internal temp 137F. Oven 225F. Pull from the oven at 125F. Sear 60 to 90 seconds per side.

## How to use this skill

Give this file to your personal agent and ask it to ping you at each step time. The agent sets up one ping per step, at the time listed, with the exact message listed. The pings go to you, the person cooking. The agent does not cook for you and does not act on your behalf. Skip any step whose time has already passed.

## Recipe

### Salt

Kosher salt, about 1/2 tsp Diamond Crystal per pound (1/4 tsp if Morton or table salt). Pat the steak dry, salt generously on all sides, set uncovered on a rack in the fridge. Flaky finishing salt is too coarse for a two-hour brine, save it for the table.

### Method

1. Pat dry, salt generously on all sides with kosher salt, set uncovered on a rack in the fridge.
2. Preheat oven to 225F.
3. Insert the Combustion predictive thermometer probe sideways, tip dead center. Set target to 137F.
4. Steak on rack, into the oven, straight from the fridge. No need to bring it to room temp.
5. Pull when the probe reads 125F, about 45 to 60 minutes.
6. Rest ten minutes while the heaviest pan gets ripping hot.
7. Thin film of high-smoke-point oil. Sear 60 to 90 seconds per side.
8. Add a knob of butter, baste for the last 30 seconds.
9. Remove, dust Bullock's Meats Ultimate Steakhouse Prime Rib Steak Seasoning over both sides. Pepper now.
10. Rest five minutes. Slice against the grain, serve.

### Seasoning notes

Bullock's is an additive, not a substitute for the salt. Salt is only its fourth ingredient, so it under-salts on its own, and its cane sugar and dehydrated garlic scorch in the sear pan. It goes on after the sear, while the steak rests, or a pinch in the butter baste.

### Why 137F

A thick-cut ribeye's fat renders best around 137F. The pull is 10 to 15F below target, and the sear and rest carry it the rest of the way.

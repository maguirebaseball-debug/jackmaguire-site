Things to do

01

Schönbrunn Palace and gardens

The palace is open daily from 08:30 to 17:00 on the winter schedule. The park opens at 06:30 and is free to enter. Take the U4 to Schönbrunn.

Current opening times

02

Upper Belvedere

See Klimt's The Kiss and the palace collection. The current listing shows daily hours of 09:00 to 18:00.

Visit and tickets

03

Vienna State Opera standing room

Same-day online sales start at 10:00. Any remaining tickets go on sale at the box office 80 minutes before the performance. The opera lists 435 standing places, with prices from €7 to €18.

Ticket information

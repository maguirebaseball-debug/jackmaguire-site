The most common mistake travelers make in Graz is going to the central train station to catch the bus to Bratislava.

FlixBus Route 885 does not depart from Graz Hauptbahnhof. It leaves from Graz P&R Webling, a park-and-ride lot on the southwestern highway fringe of the city. 

Missing this distinction means missing your departure. Taking the direct coach from Webling remains the fastest ground route between Graz and Slovakia without backtracking through Vienna.

## Graz Departure Points Compared

Here is the difference between Graz Hauptbahnhof and the actual FlixBus boarding hub at P&R Webling.

| Feature | Graz Hauptbahnhof (Hbf) | Graz P&R Webling (FlixBus Hub) |
|---|---|---|
| Location | Europaplatz, 8020 Graz (Central) | Erna-Diez-Strasse, 8054 Graz (Southwest) |
| Distance from City Center | 2 km (7 min by tram) | 5.5 km (25 min by city bus or 12 min taxi) |
| Public Transit Access | Tram lines 1, 3, 6, 7 | City Bus lines 32, 64, 65 |
| Services Available | Full rail station, cafes, lockers | Bus shelter, parking lot, basic restrooms |
| Routes Hosted | Local trains, Railjets, OBB buses | Long-distance cross-border coaches (Route 885) |

## Getting from Central Graz to P&R Webling

Do not leave your departure to the last minute. Build in thirty minutes to transfer from the historic center out to Webling.

From Jakominiplatz (the main city bus interchange in Graz), take City Bus 32 heading south toward Seiersberg. Ride roughly 18 minutes to the "Webling" stop. 

From the bus stop, walk two minutes down Erna-Diez-Strasse into the designated coach turnaround area. If you carry heavy roller bags or travel early in the morning, a taxi or rideshare from Hauptplatz to P&R Webling costs roughly 16 to 22 EUR and takes ten to twelve minutes.

The boarding area is straightforward. Look for the green FlixBus route markers and the designated loading shelter. Route 885 originates in southern Austria and pulls in roughly ten minutes before its scheduled 09:20 AM departure.

## Booking Details for Route 885

Route 885 is operated by FlixBus CEE South.

A standard ticket costs roughly $28 USD when booked a few days ahead. Booking Seat 1A on the upper level gives you front panoramic views along the Austrian autobahn and across the Burgenland wind farms towards the Slovak border.

The journey takes three hours and thirty minutes. The coach bypasses Vienna's dense inner-city traffic by staying on the outer ring roads and cutting east along the Danube basin directly into Slovakia.

## Arrival at Bratislava Mlynske Nivy

The bus terminates at Bratislava (bus station Mlynske Nivy) at Mlynske Nivy 4122/3.

Bratislava Nivy is one of the most modern bus terminals in Europe. The bus bays are located completely underground beneath a multistory retail and dining complex. 

When you exit the underground platform, escalator banks lead up into the main concourse where you will find clean automated lockers, bakeries, grocery stores, and ATM cash machines.

To reach the pedestrian core of Bratislava Old Town (Stare Mesto) and St. Martin's Cathedral:
* Walk west along Dunajska street for roughly fifteen minutes.
* Take trolleybus 40 or 60 from outside the Nivy main entrance directly to the historic gate.

Double-check your departure station on your ticket receipt before leaving your Graz hotel. Give yourself 30 minutes on Bus 32 to reach Erna-Diez-Strasse, and you will arrive at Bratislava Nivy in time for lunch.

I've been to [PARK at KIMS](https://www.instagram.com/parkatkims/) a few times, and it has very cool vibes. It's a small place, but they have cute seating. It's very aesthetic and light, with lots of natural light.

I went for the [Klover Coffee](https://klovercoffee.com/pages/about-us) pop-up and paid $15 plus tax for a Kenyan hand brew. I asked for something floral and bright, and it was extremely watery, honestly. There was a little brightness on the nose and on the tongue, mostly at the front, but ultimately it was just a very watery cup of coffee.

It wasn't necessarily bad or bitter. It just wasn't very interesting, and for $17, I would have expected something a lot better.

The service was quite nice, but the coffee really wasn't that great.

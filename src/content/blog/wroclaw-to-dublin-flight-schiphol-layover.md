Flying between regional Polish airports and Ireland usually leaves travelers with two options: a cramped direct ultra-low-cost flight on Ryanair, or a hub connection on a legacy carrier.

Connecting through Amsterdam Schiphol (AMS) on KLM is often the better route if you value reliable schedules, decent seat pitch, and checked baggage handling.

However, flying from Poland to Ireland via the Netherlands has one critical logistical wrinkle: you are exiting the Schengen Area at Schiphol. Here is how to manage a two-hour transfer smoothly.

## Flight Route and Schedule Breakdown

Here is the exact timetable and aircraft profile for the morning connection from Wroclaw to Dublin via Amsterdam.

| Flight Leg | Flight Number | Departure Time | Arrival Time | Aircraft / Operator | Seat |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wroclaw (WRO) to Amsterdam (AMS) | KL1336 | 06:00 AM | 07:45 AM | Embraer 190 (KLM Cityhopper) | 05A |
| Amsterdam (AMS) to Dublin (DUB) | KL1137 | 09:45 AM | 10:25 AM | Boeing 737 (KLM Royal Dutch Airlines) | 12A |

## Departing Copernicus Airport Wroclaw

Copernicus Airport Wroclaw (WRO) is compact and straightforward. 

For the 06:00 AM departure on KL1336, arrive at the terminal by 04:30 AM. Security wait times rarely exceed fifteen minutes at that hour. 

KLM Cityhopper uses regional Embraer jets on this route. If you carry a rollaboard suitcase near the maximum size limit, the gate agents may tag it for cargo gate-check free of charge. You can keep a personal laptop backpack with you at Seat 05A.

## The Two-Hour Transfer at Amsterdam Schiphol

KL1336 touches down at Schiphol around 07:45 AM. Cityhopper flights often park at the apron stands near Pier B or C, where a quick bus shuttles passengers directly into the terminal concourse.

This leaves you with roughly one hour and thirty minutes before gate boarding begins on the connecting leg.

### The Schengen Exit Border Control

Because Poland and the Netherlands are inside the Schengen zone while Ireland is not, you must clear passport control to exit the Schengen area at Amsterdam.

Do not stop for coffee before clearing passport control:
1. Follow the yellow overhead airport signs pointing toward "Gates D and E (Non-Schengen)".
2. You will reach the central passport control hall between the B/C Schengen concourses and the D/E Non-Schengen piers.
3. If you hold a biometric US, EU, or UK passport, use the automated e-gates (Self-Service Passport Control). Look at the camera screen and scan your passport page. The automated gate takes under twenty seconds.
4. If the e-gates have a long queue, manual agent booths operate to the right. 

In early mornings, the border wait typically runs between five and fifteen minutes.

## Boarding KL1137 to Dublin

Once through passport control, you enter the international duty-free concourse. 

Gates for Dublin flights typically depart from Pier D. Boarding for KL1137 begins at 09:17 AM, and gates close firmly at 09:30 AM for the 09:45 AM departure. 

The flight time to Dublin is one hour and forty minutes. Because Ireland has its own border controls within the Common Travel Area, you will clear Irish immigration (Garda National Immigration Bureau) upon landing at Dublin Airport Terminal 1.

Two hours at Schiphol is the ideal transfer window for this route. Clear Schengen border control immediately after deplaning, and you will reach the Dublin departure gate with twenty minutes to spare.

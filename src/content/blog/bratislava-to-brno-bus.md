Moving between Slovakia's capital and Czechia's second city is one of the easiest short journeys in Central Europe.

While many travelers default to the train between Bratislava and Brno, taking the direct coach to Brno Hotel Grand has one distinct advantage: it drops you directly on the pedestrian threshold of the historic center without navigating the crowded main railway concourse.

FlixBus Route 260 covers the distance in one hour and thirty-five minutes.

## Bus vs Train from Bratislava to Brno

## Departing from Bratislava Nivy

FlixBus Route 260 departs from Bratislava bus station Mlynske Nivy.

The digital overhead boards in the underground bus terminal display the exact platform number fifteen minutes prior to boarding. The coach originates further south and continues past Brno toward Prague and Berlin.

Drivers verify your passport or EU ID before boarding because the bus crosses the Slovak-Czech border near Breclav. Most journeys roll through without any border halt.

## Arriving at Brno Hotel Grand

The bus terminates in Brno at the bus terminal right outside Grand Hotel Brno on Benesova 603/2 (Platforms 2A, 3, or 3A).

This is the premier arrival spot in Brno:
* You are 150 meters from the central tram hub at Hlavni Nadrazi.
* You can walk up the gentle incline to Capuchin Square, Silingrovo namesti, and Petrov Cathedral in under eight minutes.
* You avoid the construction and pedestrian bottlenecks around the main railway station underpasses.

## Brno Local Transit and Fares

Brno has an exceptionally clean and punctual public transport network operated by DPMB. 

You do not need to buy paper tickets at street kiosks. Every tram and bus in Brno is equipped with contactless payment card readers at every door (Beep & Go). Simply tap your credit card, debit card, or smartphone when you board.

Here are the standard single-trip fares published by the city:

| Ticket Type | Duration | Cost (CZK) | Approx Cost (USD) | Best Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Short hop | 15 minutes | 20 CZK | ~$0.90 | 2 to 4 tram stops across the center |
| Standard trip | 60 minutes | 25 CZK | ~$1.10 | Connecting between neighborhoods |
| Extended trip | 90 minutes | 27 CZK | ~$1.20 | Longer cross-town transfers |
| All-day pass | 24 hours | 100 CZK | ~$4.40 | Full day of sightseeing or cafe hopping |

The tap system calculates the best rate automatically. If you take multiple short trips within twenty-four hours, the system caps your daily charge at the 24-hour rate.

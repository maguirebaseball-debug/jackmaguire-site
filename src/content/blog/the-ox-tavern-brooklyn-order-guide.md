The Ox Tavern sits at 1715 Foster Avenue in Ditmas Park. For two people, order the crispy Brussels sprouts, the Good Ol' Boy burger, and the quesabirria tacos. The burger comes with fries. The food total is about $51 before tax and tip.

## The order

| Item | What to ask for |
| :--- | :--- |
| Crispy Brussels sprouts | Chipotle mayo on the side |
| Good Ol' Boy burger | Medium rare, cut in half, fries extra crispy |
| Quesabirria tacos | As served, with the consommé |

Ask for a small side of chimichurri if it is available. It is good on the fries, especially after a dip in the birria consommé.

## Why this order works

The Good Ol' Boy is the burger to get if you want the richest option. It has sharp cheddar, smoked bacon, and sautéed mushrooms. Splitting one is enough once the tacos and fries arrive.

The quesabirria tacos bring a crisp corn tortilla, braised beef, Oaxaca cheese, and a cup of consommé. They also give you something useful to do with the fries. Dip them in the broth, then the chimichurri.

The Brussels sprouts keep the meal from becoming nothing but beef, cheese, and potatoes. The togarashi and lime add heat and acidity. Keeping the mayo on the side helps the fried leaves stay crisp.

## How to pace it

Have the sprouts come out first, then order the burger and tacos. This is more useful than asking the kitchen to land everything at once and watching the burger cool while you finish the starter.

An IPA, a mezcalita, or club soda with lime all fit the meal. If you are going for happy hour, check the current hours before you leave.

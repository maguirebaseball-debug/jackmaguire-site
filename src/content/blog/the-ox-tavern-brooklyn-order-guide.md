For two people, order:

1. Crispy Brussels sprouts, with the chipotle mayo on the side.
2. Good Ol' Boy burger, medium rare and cut in half, with the fries extra crispy.
3. Quesabirria tacos with the consommé.

Ask for a side of chimichurri if it is available. Dip the fries in the consommé, then the chimichurri. The food total is about $51 before tax and tip.

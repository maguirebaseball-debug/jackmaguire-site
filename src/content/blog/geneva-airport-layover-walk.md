Sitting inside Geneva Airport (GVA) during a multi-hour layover is a waste of time.

Geneva has one of the best airport-to-city rail links in the world. Trains run from the basement of the terminal directly into central Geneva (Gare de Cornavin) every few minutes, and the ride takes exactly six minutes.

With a three to four hour layover, you can easily walk the Lake Geneva shoreline, see the Jet d'Eau, eat lunch, and return through airport security without stress.

## Layover Time Budget

Here is a realistic timeline for a three-hour window between landing and re-entering security.

| Elapsed Time | Activity | Location |
|---|---|---|
| 0h 00m | Clear customs / luggage lockers | Geneva Airport Terminal 1 |
| 0h 15m | Board train to city center | Geneve-Aeroport railway platform |
| 0h 25m | Exit station and start walking | Gare de Cornavin (Place de Cornavin) |
| 0h 40m | Lakefront promenade and harbor view | Quai du Mont-Blanc |
| 1h 00m | Coffee or snack on the pier | Bains des Paquis |
| 1h 45m | Old Town bridge crossing / SIM card pickup | Pont du Mont-Blanc & Rue de Berne |
| 2h 15m | Catch return train to airport | Gare de Cornavin Platform 2 or 3 |
| 2h 30m | Arrive at terminal and clear security | Geneva Airport Departure Gates |

## Getting from the Airport to Gare Cornavin

Follow the railway signs inside Geneva Airport. The train station is integrated directly into the terminal concourse, about a three-minute walk from baggage claim.

Every train departing the airport stops at Geneva Cornavin (Gare de Cornavin), so you do not need to check destination boards. Just hop on the first train leaving. 

A single second-class ticket costs 3.00 CHF and can be purchased at the SBB ticket machines or through the SBB mobile app using contactless card payment.

## The Walking Route: Cornavin to Bains des Paquis

Exit Gare Cornavin through the main front glass doors facing Place de Cornavin. 

If you need a local Swiss or European SIM card with unthrottled data, walk across the tram plaza into the Interdiscount electronics shop or the Salt store on Rue de Lausanne. You can activate a prepaid SIM card on the spot in under ten minutes with your passport.

From Place de Cornavin, walk downhill along Rue du Mont-Blanc for six blocks. In less than eight minutes, the street opens onto Lake Geneva (Lac Leman) and the Quai du Mont-Blanc.

Turn left and follow the tree-lined pedestrian promenade along the water toward Bains des Paquis:
* You get an unobstructed view of the Jet d'Eau erupting 140 meters into the air across the harbor.
* Bains des Paquis is a public 1930s bathhouse jetty jutting into the lake. It has open-air wooden benches, a cafe serving coffee and fresh fondue, and views back toward the snow-capped Alps.
* It is casual, unpretentious, and completely free of luxury retail markup.

## Returning to the Airport

Give yourself at least 90 minutes before your departure flight time if traveling carry-on only with an airline like easyJet, or two hours if checking bags.

Walk back up Rue du Mont-Blanc to Gare Cornavin. Board any outbound train marked "Geneve-Aeroport". Trains leave roughly every ten to twelve minutes from platforms 2, 3, or 4.

Six minutes later, you step off the train directly beneath the departure check-in hall.

Skip the airport lounge. Take the six-minute train into Cornavin, walk straight to Bains des Paquis for coffee by the water, and head back with plenty of time to clear security.

Tsumo Omakase, 132 E 28th St, Kips Bay. Sunday lunch: $48, 11 courses, 60 minutes.

![The Tsumo storefront at 132 E 28th St, with bamboo screens and subtle signage that is easy to walk past](/images/tsumo-sunday-lunch-omakase/tsumo-storefront.webp)

Bottom line: arrive five minutes early, eat the set in order, and put any extra dollars toward a second otoro.

1. **Let the order work.** Eleven courses run lean to rich: madai and hamachi first, otoro and the toro handroll last. Don't reshuffle it. The salt and acid up front sharpen the fat at the end.
2. **The best bite is the cheapest upgrade.** Otoro with caviar is the piece reviewers name most. A second otoro ($16) or uni ($10) à la carte beats the wagyu add-on.
3. **Go light on soy.** Dip fish-side down, once. The rice is already seasoned; drowning it flattens the balance and the salt stacks up fast.
4. **Drink one thing.** There's no pairing, so pick a single clean sake and stay with it. One glass keeps the palate sharp through course eleven.
5. **Expect sixty minutes and no dessert.** The set ends at the handroll, and lunch often leaves you short of full. Plan the next stop; don't fill up here.

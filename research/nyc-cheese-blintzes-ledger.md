# NYC cheese blintzes evidence ledger

Research frozen on 2026-08-20. This is an internal reporting artifact, not article copy.

## Scope and rules

- Geography: New York City only.
- Dish: a current cheese-filled blintz, naleśnik, or nalysnyk available to an ordinary customer.
- Exclusion pack: Michelin Guide New York, Eater New York, and The Infatuation New York.
- Exclusion unit: the restaurant, not the dish. Any confirmed editorial coverage by a frozen-pack publication removes the restaurant.
- Discovery evidence is not quality evidence. A current menu establishes availability. Dish-specific reviews, owner descriptions, recent ordering data, and repeated independent recommendations support ranking confidence.
- No firsthand claims. Jack has not eaten these dishes.
- Prices are snapshots and must be presented as subject to change.

## Ranking constraints

- The final 25 come from the first 25 rows marked `Rank`.
- High-confidence candidates can receive fuller entries and stronger superlatives.
- Medium-confidence candidates can be ranked comparatively, but copy must stay close to verifiable menu facts.
- Medium-low candidates need short, practical entries and a price or availability caveat.
- No entry may say or imply that Jack tasted the dish.
- The page should say that menus and prices change and should link readers to the current menu or order page.
- Do not include a methodology section in the published article.

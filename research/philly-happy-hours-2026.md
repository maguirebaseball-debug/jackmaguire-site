# Philadelphia happy hours, 2026 research

Updated and accessed: 2026-09-03

## Scope and editorial read

This is a current research pass for Philadelphia proper, with an emphasis on drinks plus a meaningful food deal. I prioritized official venue pages and current menus. A secondary listing is used only to surface a lead or corroborate a citywide program.

The strongest page candidates are:

1. [Sampan and Graffiti Bar](https://www.sampanphilly.com/menu): daily 4-7pm, $5 drink features, and a large small-plates menu mostly at $8-$9.
2. [Double Knot](https://www.doubleknotphilly.com/menu): daily 4-7pm, $5-$9 food, $5 beer, $6 wine, and $7 cocktails.
3. [El Camino Real](https://elcaminophilly.com/): weekday 5-7pm, $3 beer, half-price margaritas, and $5 appetizers four days a week.
4. [National Mechanics](https://www.nationalmechanics.com/): weekday 4-7pm, a broad $5-$8 food and drink menu, plus late-night specials.
5. [Osteria](https://www.osteriaphilly.com/): the best quality-focused option in this set, with a long bar-only food menu and $7-$9 drinks.
6. [Loch Bar](https://lochbar.com/philly/our-menus/): weekday 3-6pm, $2 oysters, $5 shrimp, and $6 beer.

For a simple site page, I would publish about 12 rows, use one short note above the table, and put the remaining leads in an expandable "More places to check" section. The deals should be labeled as verified on 2026-09-03 because prices and schedules change often.

### Neighborhood takeaways

This is an editorial inference from the current deal mix, not a claim that one neighborhood is objectively better. Fishtown has the strongest concentration of cheap food-and-drink combinations, especially at Fette Sau, Dock Street, Frankford Hall, and Picnic. University City has the best cluster of polished bar or patio options, with CO-OP, White Dog, Louie Louie, and Corio. Northern Liberties has the broadest spread of time windows, from Yanaga’s Monday all-day offer through Jerry’s 4-7pm happy hour.

- For a first version, the best table columns are: Venue, neighborhood, hours, drinks, food, restrictions, and links.

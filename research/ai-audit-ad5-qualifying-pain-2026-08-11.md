# AI Audit Ad 5: Qualifying Pain

Date: 2026-08-11

## Decision

Create a problem-aware Meta creative for people who recognize a repeated computer task but have not yet chosen a practical AI workflow. The creative must qualify the audience, make delay feel costly without fake scarcity, show the one-task plan as the solution, and explain the deliverable mechanism.

The ad uses four beats:

1. Qualification question: Does the same computer task show up every week?
2. Cost of persistence: leaving it alone keeps charging the buyer in pieces of the workday.
3. Solution: one task, one practical plan.
4. Mechanism: current steps, projected savings, software cost, and first test.

The cost-of-delay language is a practical inference from research on fragmented work and task interruptions. It does not claim a specific productivity loss for every buyer. The $59 price, next-business-day delivery, and refund condition come from the live offer in `src/pages/Your-AI-Audit.astro`.

## Copy

Primary text, 420 characters:

> Does the same computer task show up every week? If it takes 45+ minutes, leaving it alone means paying for it again and again in pieces of your workday. Send me one task. I’ll select one practical AI workflow and show the current steps, projected savings, software cost, and first test in a one-page plan. $59, due by the next business day after intake, or a full refund if I can’t support a 30-minute weekly projection.

Headline, 31 characters:

> Still Paying for the Same Task?

Description, 23 characters:

> One task. One $59 plan.

## Framework application

- Awareness: problem-aware cold social audience, moving one step toward solution awareness.
- Qualification: the 45+ minute recurring computer-task condition matches the landing page's stated good-fit threshold.
- FOMO: consequence framing based on repeated weekly cost, with no false deadline, scarcity, or unsupported savings claim.
- Solution: one human-selected no-code or built-in AI workflow.
- Mechanism: the one-page plan makes the current workflow, projected savings, software cost, setup path, and first test explicit.

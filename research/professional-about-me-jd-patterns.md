# Professional About Page, Role Pattern Review

Date: 2026-07-28

## Scope

Reviewed 47 job-specific resume records in the ResumeAutomation project, along with the canonical base resume, confirmed skills inventory, project memory, and current state. The tailored summaries and skills sections were used as the closest consistent record of what each job description prioritized.

Role titles were concentrated in paid social and paid media, with a smaller group of growth, performance marketing, social media, and integrated marketing roles.

## Page strategy

The page should answer the dominant hiring questions in this order:

1. Can Jack own paid social at meaningful scale?
2. Can he connect platform performance to profit and downstream customer quality?
3. Can he build a reliable measurement system around CAPI, CRM data, BI, holdouts, and LTV?
4. Can he run a repeatable creative testing program?
5. Can he lead people and partners while remaining hands-on?
6. Can he communicate clearly with Finance, executives, creative, product, legal, and engineering?
7. Does his AI use improve decisions and operating speed?

## Evidence selected for the page

- 12+ years in performance marketing
- $30M+ in managed media spend
- More than $7M in career-wide profitable revenue, including an estimated $5M at National Debt Relief
- $10M annual paid social budget at National Debt Relief
- Paid Social BI dashboard, custom LTV and KPI models, CAPI, offline events, and holdout testing
- Fortune 100 work across 17,000 Dollar General locations
- 10,000+ leads across a 500-office Visiting Angels network
- 150,000+ email leads and 30x social growth at Ascension
- Team leadership, agency leadership, vendor management, and executive presentations
- Daily AI-assisted work in analysis, creative testing, reporting, and workflow automation

## Design direction

Use the site's olive and paper palette, but make the page feel like a field notebook crossed with a performance dashboard. The visual system should be precise, warm, practical, and personal. Avoid standard portfolio cards, glossy corporate gradients, platform-logo walls, and inflated claims. Keep the existing site header and footer.

The page should include a direct point of view, quantified proof, a career record, working principles, selected professional writing, a small human note, LinkedIn, and email.

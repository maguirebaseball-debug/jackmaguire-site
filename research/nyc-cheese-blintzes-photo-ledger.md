# NYC cheese blintzes photo ledger

Checked August 21, 2026.

## Publication gate

A photo is accepted only when all three conditions hold:

1. Identity: it clearly depicts the named restaurant, its legible signage, or its actual blintz.
2. Source: it comes from the restaurant's official site, the hosting institution's official page, or an authorized platform embed. Ordinary search-result, delivery-app, review-site, and publisher photos are not enough.
3. Quality: it is sharp and useful at article width. Ambiguous street frames, generic food, logos without a place, and weak crops are excluded.

The article uses a small editorial selection with a linked source credit. It does not imply that the credited source licenses the image for unrelated reuse.

## Rejected source classes

- Wikimedia Commons: no exact usable result for any of the 25; broad searches returned false positives.
- Openverse: no exact reusable result except a Manhattan Diner Flickr photo under CC BY-NC 2.0. The noncommercial restriction was treated as too ambiguous for publication here.
- KartaView and Mapillary: rights-cleared street imagery existed near five restaurants, but inspected frames did not clearly show the relevant storefront signage.
- Delivery and review platforms: useful for menu verification, not treated as permission to republish their photos.
- News and local publisher photos: exact images were found for Retro Polish and Baku Palace, but rejected in favor of official sources or no photo.
- Generic blintz photography: excluded because it would falsely imply restaurant-specific evidence.
